HOW Q-SHIELD WORKS

THE PROBLEM

Field operators need to be seen

In remote or high-risk environments, command must know each operator's status — location, movement, and health — in real time, even when connectivity is poor and adversaries may be listening.

STEP 1 — OBSERVE

Sensors capture everything

A wrist-mounted Raspberry Pi reads GPS coordinates, an IMU accelerometer for motion and impacts, and a pulse-oximeter for heart rate — forming a compact status snapshot every two seconds.

STEP 2 — DETECT

Smart status classification

On-device logic classifies each reading: ACTIVE (normal), DISTRESS (elevated stress), or INJURED_SUSPECTED (sudden impact with abnormal vitals). Priority escalates automatically.

STEP 3 — EXPLAIN

Human-readable events

Every status change generates a plain-English event log entry explaining what happened and why the system flagged it — so operators trust the system's decisions.

STEP 4 — RESPOND

Command acts with confidence

This dashboard shows all unit data at a glance — status, location trail, vitals trend, and event history — enabling faster, better-informed decisions in critical moments.

🔐 Why Post-Quantum Cryptography?

Adversaries are already capturing encrypted military and emergency communications today with the intent to decrypt them once quantum computers become powerful enough — a strategy called "harvest now, decrypt later." Q-SHIELD integrates NIST-standardized post-quantum algorithms (ML-KEM for encryption, ML-DSA for signatures) so that status packets are protected against both current and future threats. Even data intercepted today will remain confidential decades from now.
